About the App

Budgeting Tool is a simple budgeting app made for Filipinos to track daily spending and save money.

Features

  • Expense Tracker
  • Savings Goals
  • Offline Mode

How It Works

  1. Set your monthly income
  2. Log your daily expenses
  3. Track your savings progress
